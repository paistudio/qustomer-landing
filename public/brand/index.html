Accordion (FAQ)

Is Qustomer software or a service?

A service. You do not buy software or learn a new tool. We set up the system for your business and run your customer conversations with you.

Which channels do you support?

WhatsApp is our main channel. Website forms and chat, social media and email feed the same customer record.
